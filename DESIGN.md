# Portfolio visual system

The visual metaphor is an engineering atlas: product evidence, system relationships, and measured public activity. The first viewport establishes the engineer and the kind of problems he owns. Project studies carry the middle of the page. Analytics substantiate activity without pretending to measure private company impact.

Retain the reference's midnight ground (#0d0e16), cyan (#22d3ee), violet (#a78bfa), and selective pink (#f472b6). Use pale ink for primary content and muted blue-gray for secondary labels. Use gradients only for the guide's deliberate identity reveal and thin frame accents. Keep the surrounding canvas quiet.

Space Grotesk is the display and reading face. JetBrains Mono is reserved for measurements, code fragments, and diagram notation. Favor large names, clear technical headlines, compact factual copy, and aligned numerals over slogans and repeated badges.

Each region has a distinct composition: editorial portrait hero, connected system diagram, orbiting technology field, physical ID with activity plots, three product studies, contribution city, and concise contact footer. Avoid repeating one card-grid composition throughout.

Motion describes behavior: reveal the name, trace dependencies, send a data packet through a workflow, orbit dependencies, show a clock advancing, scan an object list, and dissolve/restore a camera effect. Avoid constant unrelated pulsing. Default frames remain complete when animation is disabled; reduced motion shows a finished readable state.

Keep the guide's portrait fallback, hobby carousel, technology orbit and satellites, lanyard badge, public stats, projects, and daily city. Use an editorial portrait in an open-collar shirt and navy overshirt throughout. Do not use the rejected hoodie-and-laptop motif. A laptop, if needed in future artwork, must be a MacBook Pro. Personal interests occupy one small supporting region. The cover and contact copy contain no personal email.

Every SVG is independently renderable offline. Verify complete loops and reduced motion at desktop and mobile widths. Product screenshots must come from the owner's public repositories; label conceptual system diagrams as architecture rather than product screenshots.
